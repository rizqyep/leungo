Leungo needs the BlackHole audio driver

Leungo listens to your music through a virtual loopback device. It is not installed on this Mac yet.

  1. Install the driver (macOS asks for your password).
  2. Restart your Mac if the installer says so. Leungo continues by itself once the driver appears.
  3. In System Settings, Sound, choose BlackHole 2ch as the output.
